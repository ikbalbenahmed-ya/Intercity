Method & notes

  • Cohort: drivers with 2+ intercity posts since 21 July, pickup in Algiers, Oran, Constantine, Tizi Ouzou, Béjaïa, Sétif.
  • Figures are medians (the typical driver) — they won't aggregate like averages. Net earnings = instant-ride take-home only.
  • Trade-off measured within each driver (intercity days vs their own normal days); a day counts only where a trip was confirmed.
  • Online-per-day excludes days a driver never went online. Services shown are services used. Delivery, test rides, and the noisy fraud flag are excluded.
